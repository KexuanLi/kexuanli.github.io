Kexuan Li

Home Research

I am a statistician at Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS).

My research interests span asymptotic theory, nonparametric statistics, survival analysis, deep learning, clinical trials, and quantum computing.

My industry work includes statistical contributions to global regulatory submissions and approvals for tofersen (Qalsody) in SOD1-ALS (FDA) and deucravacitinib (Sotyktu) in psoriatic arthritis (FDA), as well as pivotal study readouts for cendakimab in eosinophilic esophagitis.
